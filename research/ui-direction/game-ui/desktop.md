# Desktop-resident character UI: prior art for YUI

Scope: games and apps where a character or a fake/real desktop hosts the UI. Read-only browsing with ego-browser on 2026-11-22. Screenshots are in `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-nam-young-woo-Desktop-codes-waifu-2026-YUI/2e007b0a-8144-4c22-8bc2-f8dee23bf883/scratchpad/game-ui/desktop/<slug>.png` (18 files, each a 1548x932 capture of the page named in its entry).

Method and limits, read before relying on this:
- Most Steam captures show the store page, not the game. In-game UI is visible only in the thumbnail strip, which is too small to read text from. Visual claims below are marked "thumb" when they come from thumbnails and "text" when they come from the store description or a wiki.
- Steam store pages were captured while signed out. No purchases, sign-ins, or forms.
- Not verified in this pass: the Doki Doki Literature Club meta/desktop tricks (the Steam page has no such text, and I did not open a wiki section), the Persona 5 phone/chat UI (only the Steam capsule was captured), and The Sims 4 pie menu (the Sims fandom page for it does not exist under that name, and the Steam capture shows gameplay, not the menu). These three have no dedicated PNG.
- Wikipedia leads for Needy Streamer Overload, KinitoPET, Desktop Mate, VPet and Her Story came back empty, so their descriptions come from Steam.

## Patterns across entries

- The character is either on top of windows (Desktop Mate, VPet, Bongo Cat, Dejinochi) or in a corner window (My Galaxy Buddy, Amber).
- Notifications are inbox-style lists or chat windows, not toasts (Hypnospace, Emily, Simulacra).
- Affection and stats are shown as counters in a corner or on a device (Galaxy Buddy timer and currency, Bongo Cat points, Dejinochi album) or hidden in choices (Needy, Emily, DDLC).
- Right-click menus are rare in this set. Most interaction is drag, click, or a pet on the cursor.
- Closest tech match is MateEngine (VRM desktop companion) and Amber (AI companion with voice, memory and greetings). Amber is 18+ and offers an always-listening mode.

## What YUI could borrow

Paths follow the layout in AGENTS.md (`src/<layer>/`) and the docs it lists. Existence of each file was not checked in this pass; treat the paths as pointers to check.

1. Perch on the active window's title bar (Desktop Mate). When the user drags the pet onto a window, it sits on that window's top edge and follows it. Put the behaviour in a module under `src/renderer/` or the pet window's feature folder under `src/windows/`, and keep it out of the composing file. Idle wander can reuse `docs/guide/motions.md`.
2. Cursor reaction that needs no brain (Desktop Mate, Bongo Cat). A pet that chases or nudges the cursor, or punches the taskbar on key presses, is a client-side reaction. It fires without choosing words, which matches "firing ≠ judgment" in AGENTS.md. Live under `src/ambient/`.
3. Inbox-style notices (Hypnospace inbox, Emily IM). Proactive messages and Threads land in a small list the user opens, not a stream of toasts. Map to `docs/reference/threads.md` and the `src/ui/` notice surface; cap the list and let the user clear it.
4. Corner status chip that can collapse (My Galaxy Buddy). A small chip with the connection state or the persona name, collapsible with one button. Keep it in `src/ui/`, and show nothing when all is well.
5. Short pet context menu (Desktop Mate, VPet, NookPhone app grid). Right-click on the character opens five items at most: talk, settings, mute, hide, quit. Put the menu in its own module under `src/ui/` so the pet window only routes events.
6. Away and return state (Emily is Away away messages, Dejinochi album). When the user is idle, the pet shows an "away" look or a one-line away message, and its greeting on return comes from card text, not from the client writing words. Tie to `src/ambient/` and the greeting path in `docs/reference/client-context.md`.
7. Labelled listening modes (Amber "Continuous Listening" vs "Text Input" vs "Voice Input"). Any mic or screen-watch mode is a named, visible setting with a clear on-state indicator, and it defaults off. Put it under `src/settings/` and the settings window in `src/windows/`.
8. Phone-style chat history opened from the pet (Simulacra, NookPhone). Chat history opens as a compact phone-shaped panel from the pet instead of a separate app window, so chat stays one surface. Keep it in `src/ui/`; do not add a second window.

## What to avoid

- Fake OS chrome that could be mistaken for real system UI (KinitoPET, Hypnospace, Needy's pastel desktop frames). YUI's notices and bubbles must never look like OS toasts or dialogs. Use the YUI pet's own bubble style and say where the message came from.
- Pushy stats and nagging (Needy's follower count, Bongo Cat's rarity and hat drops, Galaxy Buddy's currency). YUI has no points or streak counters. Proactive speech stays rare and skippable.
- Horror framing and jump scares (KinitoPET, Simulacra, DDLC's meta tricks). The character never fakes errors, glitches or "she is watching you" effects.
- Always-listening by default and 18+ companion framing (Amber). Microphone and screen watch stay opt-in, labelled, and off on first launch.
- Right-click menus with many items or submenus (keep to five, see idea 5).
- Windows that stack on top of the user's real windows and stay there (Desktop Mate's perch is a drag the user chooses; the pet never grabs focus or moves on its own into someone's document).
- Client-written replies. Greetings and away lines come from card text or the backend, never from strings the client composes (AGENTS.md "firing ≠ judgment").
