Proactive page, item by item

Settings › Proactive, redesigned while it moves to React. Each item below switches between Before (the page on main) and After, in a 760px settings window that shows only the part the item changes. The last window is the whole page with every item applied.

Dotted underline proposed marks a proposed string. Every other string is today's, from src/ui/i18n/en.ts.

1 · Thread turns moves into the External signals fold

Thread reactions and External signals, both folds open. External signals is off, as it ships.

Today Thread turns sits in the Thread reactions fold, but the External signals switch in the next group locks it: it rests at 55% with its own reason line, and it is the one row that ignores its own fold's dim (controls.css:277). After, it is a row of the External signals fold, so turning External signals off dims it like every other row there, and the fold's Off summary covers it. The reason line, the 55% rule and the CSS exception go away; Thread reactions keeps only its two thresholds.

  • When it needs me → When it needs her proposed (the page speaks of her elsewhere)
  • Port and address → Thread turns, port and address proposed (the fold title names what it holds)
  • Turn on External signals to use this. → removed removed

2 · Her day, under How often she speaks up

The top of the page. Press Less, Normal or More.

Today a three-line rule paragraph sits under Less / Normal / More. After, a 24-hour strip shows the edited character's day: her active-hours spans as shaded bands with their pace, the scheduled greetings as ticks at their times, and her face at now. The short track under it is the check-in ladder after you go quiet; Less, Normal and More re-space its marks by the pace factors in spans.ts:11 (×3, ×1, ×0.5). It shows only when the client may start a turn, never what she says, and the segment now sits straight on the section, without a box around it.

  • For every character, all day, it scales the wait after a conversation … Less about 1/3 as often. → Scheduled greetings keep their times at every pace. proposed
  • Check-ins after quiet proposed (the ladder's label; the bands reuse Less / Normal / More)

3 · Workflows moves into the External signals fold

External signals on, its fold open, one saved workflow. Try Add workflow and the × on hover.

Workflows is not something she starts on her own, yet today it is its own section with an add form that is always open. After, it sits in the External signals fold under the port and address, since a workflow normally answers through a signal: the list, then an Add workflow row that opens the Label and URL fields in place. The fields get the inset Hairline the number fields already have, and Delete asks first, with focus on Cancel, as check-in and greeting rows do.

  • Port and address → Port, address and workflows proposed
  • Add (the always-open form's button) → Add workflow proposed; the opened form keeps Add, with Cancel beside it

4 · One number column, and copy fixes

Quiet for a while off, Screen changes on, both folds and Advanced open; the rail's tooltip shown.

Today each number field is as wide as its largest value and its unit slot varies, so the inputs start anywhere in a 40px spread (616 to 656px on main, with the bundled font). After, every number row on the page takes one 88px field and a 48px unit slot, empty when there is no unit, so every input shares one left and one right edge. Hourly limits put the total first. In code the first two limits count the same turns and urgent signals count in neither (guardrails.ts:70-107), so they are not a sum, and with the shipped 24 and 40 the total never trips; the labels below say what each one counts.
