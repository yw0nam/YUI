# Settings

The settings window opens from **Settings…** in the tray menu, or from **All settings…** on her quick card. The strip at the top picks the character the window edits. The rail has two groups, the pages for the character you edit and the pages for every character, and the search box above it highlights matching rows, counts them on each page, and dims a page with no match.

![Settings window, Connection page, Chat section with a provider and a model filled in](/shots/settings-connection.png)

| Page | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Connection | Chat, speech recognition, and speech synthesis for this character ([Connect a backend](connect-a-backend.md), [Voice in and out](voice.md)) |
| Voice | How she speaks, and the message window that speech and typing can move to, which is off outside the desktop app and while unified chat shows ([Voice in and out](voice.md)) |
| Persona | Her card ([Cards](cards.md)) |
| Body | The model that renders her, her relations, and the camera |
| History | The conversation, session by session, on this device, with the context meter and delegated tasks; a reset keeps the earlier turns |
| Together | What happens with several characters on the desktop; Relations off hides the sliders and keeps the stored values |
| Listening and bubble | Her ears and the speech bubble; the reading dictionary is case-sensitive and the longest word wins |
| Permissions | What she may see of your screen |
| Plugins | MCP servers and skills for every character's backend ([Plugins](plugins.md)) |
| Proactive | When she speaks first, and the workflows she fires on demand ([Features](features.md#proactive-and-context)) |
| Motion and desktop | How she moves, climbs, falls, and spends power |
| General | The settings for this device, Language, Appearance, Your name, and the two Help entries |

## Tips

- An empty field uses the bundled default.
- **Plugins** appears only in the desktop app, where the plugin host runs.
- The note line under the strip says where a change shows: in main, in that character's own window, or when it opens.
