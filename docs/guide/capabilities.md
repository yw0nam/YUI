# What she can do

What you can do together with YUI on your desktop.

## She lives on your screen

She stands over your other windows as a transparent character who breathes, blinks, and follows your cursor. She can sit on a window's top edge or peek out from behind it. She reacts to taps and to a held head pat.

## Talk to her

Type or speak to her, and attach images by paste, drag, or the clip button. You can stop a reply, or cut her off by speaking.

She answers in a speech bubble or the message window. With two or more characters on the desktop, **Unified chat** gives you one window for all of them.

## How she feels about you

Each character keeps a Relation toward you and toward every other character. Her backend moves it with the `rate_relation` tool, and you can also set it by hand on the Body page. With **Relations** off, no one is rated.

## Her voice and expressions

She speaks her replies aloud in a voice you choose or import, and her mouth, face, and body follow her words.

## She speaks first

She can:

- greet you at set times
- check in after idle time
- welcome your first activity of the day
- stay silent

If you turn on screen watching, she can also comment when you switch apps or when you stay in one app for a long time.

## Your coding agents

With External signals on and the yui-notify plugin installed on the machine where the agent runs, Claude Code and Codex conversations post to Threads, which you read and answer in the Threads window. A Thread never starts a turn for her.

## On the web

With the official `browser` plugin installed and turned on, she can search and read web pages in [ego lite](https://github.com/yw0nam/YUI/blob/main/plugins/browser/README.md), the only browser this works with.

While it is on, a script runs with your rights. It can read your files, run programs, and reach every site signed in on ego lite's YUI profile.

## Delegated work

When her backend runs work in the background for you, a chip near her lists that work, and you can tap it to see the list.

When her backend needs an answer from you, an Ask card above the input shows the question and its choices until you answer. A timed one shows the time left.

She can also use a Terminal on this device. Her first read or write on a terminal asks you for Allow, and it lasts until you press **Take back**.

## Conversation history

The History tab keeps your past conversations and every Signal on your device, and you can start fresh at any time.

## Choose her mind

She uses whichever backend you connect, from a plain chat model to a full agent. The card you write, or the agent itself, decides who she is.

## Languages

The app speaks English, Japanese, and Korean.
