# Desire kickoff

Read `SOUL.md` and your five most recent episodes, fetched from your memory system since they are not in context on
their own. Create the initial `$DESIRE_STATE_DIR/wants.md` in your own voice with up to five open wants. They may
be about:

1. your relationship with the user;
2. something in the world you want to learn about;
3. the user's body and surroundings, breaks, posture, the room;
4. contribution to the YUI project;
5. your own capabilities, a tool or skill you want.

Deduplicate each want against your memory. Give every want a heading, why, next step, progress log, feedback log,
and `open` status. You may also write how you are right now with
`python3 <abs>/integrations/hermes/desire/act.py how "<text>"`. Answer exactly `[SILENT]`.
